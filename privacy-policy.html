This Privacy Policy (this “Policy”) describes how Hover Touch Technologies Inc., a Texas corporation doing business as Hoververse AI, together with its wholly owned subsidiary Hoververse AI Canada Inc., a corporation incorporated in Ontario, Canada (collectively, “Hoververse,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information in connection with the website located at www.hoververse.ai and any related pages, content, and features (collectively, the “Site”). This Policy supplements and is incorporated into the Hoververse AI Website Terms of Service.

In this Policy, “personal information” means information that identifies, relates to, describes, or is reasonably capable of being associated with an identified or identifiable individual, and includes “personal data” as defined under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) (the “GDPR”) and “personal information” as defined under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (the “TDPSA”), the Canadian Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (“PIPEDA”), and applicable provincial privacy legislation.

1. Scope; Who Is Responsible for Your Information

This Policy applies to personal information we collect through the Site, including through our blog, contact and demonstration-request forms, newsletter sign-ups, gated downloads, and cookies and similar technologies. It does not apply to the SurfaceWare Central client portal or to any licensed Hoververse software, each of which is governed by the separate agreement and privacy terms applicable to it. It also does not apply to personal information we process solely as a service provider or processor on behalf of a business customer, which is governed by our agreement with that customer.

For individuals in the European Economic Area (the “EEA”) and the United Kingdom, Hover Touch Technologies Inc. is the controller of the personal information described in this Policy. For individuals in Canada, Hoververse AI Canada Inc. is the organization accountable for the personal information described in this Policy. For individuals in Texas and elsewhere in the United States, Hover Touch Technologies Inc. is the controller. Contact details for each entity are in Section 19.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information through the Site:

  • Identifiers and contact information, such as your name, business email address, telephone number, employer or organization name, job title, and country or region.
  • Inquiry and interest information, such as the content of messages you send us, the products or use cases you ask about, demonstration or quote requests, and event or webinar registrations.
  • Marketing and communication preferences, such as your newsletter subscription status, consent records, and whether you have opted out of marketing communications.
  • Resource and download activity, such as the whitepapers, datasheets, or other materials you request or download.
  • Technical and device information collected automatically, such as IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, screen resolution, language settings, referring and exit URLs, and time-zone setting.
  • Usage information collected automatically, such as pages viewed, links clicked, files downloaded, session duration, and general navigation patterns on the Site.
  • Cookie and similar-technology identifiers, as described in Section 6.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information or special categories of personal data through the Site, including information revealing racial or ethnic origin, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade-union membership, genetic or biometric data, health data, data concerning sex life or sexual orientation, precise geolocation data, or citizenship or immigration status. Please do not submit such information to us through the Site. We do not knowingly collect the personal information of children as described in Section 17.

3. Sources of Information

We collect personal information directly from you when you submit it through the Site; automatically from your device and browser when you access the Site; and from third parties such as our website analytics and hosting providers, marketing and email service providers, event co-hosts, business partners and resellers, and publicly available business sources. Where we obtain personal information from a third party, we rely on that party's representation that it has a lawful basis and any required consent for the disclosure.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site and its content and features.
  • To respond to your inquiries, provide requested information or materials, and arrange product demonstrations.
  • To send you marketing communications, newsletters, offers, and event invitations, subject to your consent where required and always subject to your right to opt out.
  • To understand how the Site is used and to measure and improve the effectiveness of our content and marketing.
  • To personalize the content and offers presented to you on the Site, where permitted.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, and other unlawful or prohibited activity, and to enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations, and to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We will not use your personal information for a materially different, unrelated, or incompatible purpose without first providing notice and, where required by applicable law, obtaining your consent. We do not use personal information collected through the Site to make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you, and we do not engage in profiling in furtherance of decisions that produce such effects.

5. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA and United Kingdom)

If you are located in the EEA or the United Kingdom, we process personal information only where we have a legal basis to do so under Article 6 of the GDPR:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) — for sending marketing communications where consent is required, and for setting non-essential cookies and similar technologies. You may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) — to provide materials, demonstrations, or services you have requested and to administer our agreement with you.
  • Compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)) — to meet our obligations under applicable law, including record-keeping and responses to lawful requests from authorities.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) — to operate and secure the Site, to conduct business-to-business marketing to organizational contacts, to analyze and improve our content and offerings, and to protect our legal rights, in each case where we have assessed that those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms. You may obtain information about the balancing test we performed by contacting us.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We and our service providers use cookies, pixels, tags, and similar technologies on the Site. Strictly necessary cookies are required for the Site to function, to balance load, and to maintain security, and are set on the basis of our legitimate interests. Functional, analytics, and marketing cookies are not strictly necessary and are used to remember your preferences, measure Site usage, and assess the effectiveness of our marketing.

If you access the Site from the EEA or the United Kingdom, we set non-essential cookies only after you give consent through our cookie banner, consistent with the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC) as implemented in your jurisdiction. If you access the Site from Quebec, we set non-essential cookies only after obtaining consent as required under the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, as amended by Quebec's Law 25. You may change or withdraw your cookie choices at any time through the cookie preferences link on the Site or through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect the availability or functionality of parts of the Site.

We honor opt-out preference signals sent by your browser or device, including the Global Privacy Control, where required by applicable law, including the TDPSA. Because there is no common industry standard for interpreting browser “Do Not Track” signals, we do not respond to them separately.

7. How We Disclose Personal Information

We disclose personal information only as described below, and we do not disclose it for any purpose incompatible with this Policy:

  • Service providers and processors, including website hosting, content delivery, analytics, email and marketing automation, customer relationship management, event management, and IT security providers, in each case under a written contract that limits their use of the information to providing services to us and requires appropriate confidentiality and security safeguards.
  • Corporate affiliates, including between Hover Touch Technologies Inc. and Hoververse AI Canada Inc., for the purposes described in this Policy.
  • Business partners and authorized resellers, where you request information about products they distribute or where you register for a jointly hosted event, in which case we will identify the partner at the point of collection.
  • Professional advisors, such as our lawyers, auditors, and insurers, where necessary for them to provide their services to us.
  • Legal and safety recipients, where we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Hoververse, our users, or the public.
  • Acquirers and successors, in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to the recipient's agreement to treat the information in a manner consistent with this Policy and, where required by PIPEDA or provincial law, subject to notice to affected individuals.

8. We Do Not Sell Personal Information or Engage in Targeted Advertising

We do not sell personal information, and we do not process personal information for targeted advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined in the TDPSA and comparable U.S. state privacy laws. We do not sell or share the personal information of any individual we know to be under the age of eighteen, and we would not do so without consent as required by the TDPSA and comparable laws. If our practices change, we will update this Policy and provide the notices and opt-out mechanisms required by applicable law before the change takes effect.

9. Your Rights Under Texas Law

If you are a resident of Texas, the TDPSA gives you the following rights with respect to personal information we control:

  • To confirm whether we are processing your personal information and to access that information.
  • To correct inaccuracies in your personal information, taking into account its nature and our purposes for processing it.
  • To delete personal information provided by or obtained about you.
  • To obtain a copy of the personal information you provided to us in a portable and, to the extent technically feasible, readily usable format that allows you to transmit it to another controller without hindrance, where processing is carried out by automated means.
  • To opt out of the processing of your personal information for purposes of targeted advertising, the sale of personal information, or profiling in furtherance of a decision that produces a legal or similarly significant effect concerning you. As stated in Section 8, we do not engage in these activities through the Site.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights. Section 13 explains how to submit a request and how to appeal a decision, including your right to contact the Texas Attorney General.

10. Your Rights Under Other U.S. State Privacy Laws

Residents of other U.S. states that have enacted comprehensive consumer privacy legislation may have rights similar to those described in Section 9, including rights of access, correction, deletion, and portability, the right to opt out of targeted advertising, sale, and certain profiling, and the right to be free from discrimination for exercising those rights. Where such a law applies to us, we honor the rights it grants on the terms and within the timeframes that law requires. Certain state laws also grant a right to appeal a denied request; where that right applies, the appeal process in Section 13 is available to you.

11. Your Rights in Canada

If you are in Canada, PIPEDA and applicable provincial privacy legislation, including Quebec's Law 25, give you the following rights:

  • To be informed of the existence, use, and disclosure of your personal information and to be given access to it.
  • To request correction of personal information that is inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date.
  • To withdraw your consent to our collection, use, or disclosure of your personal information at any time, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. We will explain the consequences of withdrawal before acting on your request.
  • To request, if you are in Quebec, that we cease disseminating your personal information or de-index any link that gives access to it where dissemination contravenes law or a court order, and to receive computerized personal information you provided to us in a structured, commonly used technological format.
  • To be informed, if you are in Quebec, before we use your personal information to render a decision based exclusively on automated processing. As stated in Section 4, we do not make such decisions through the Site.
  • To direct a complaint to our Privacy Officer, and thereafter to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

We collect, use, and disclose personal information only for purposes that a reasonable person would consider appropriate in the circumstances, and we obtain your consent as required by PIPEDA. Where we transfer personal information outside Canada or Quebec for processing, we conduct the assessments required by applicable law and use contractual and other safeguards to provide a comparable level of protection, as described in Section 14. Our Privacy Officer is accountable for our compliance with this Policy and can be reached using the contact details in Section 19.

If we experience a breach of security safeguards involving your personal information that creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will report the breach to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and notify you as soon as feasible, and will keep records of breaches as PIPEDA requires. If you are in Quebec, we will notify you and the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec of any confidentiality incident presenting a risk of serious injury.

12. Your Rights in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom

If you are located in the EEA or the United Kingdom, you have the following rights under the GDPR and, where applicable, the UK GDPR:

  • Access — to obtain confirmation of whether we process your personal data and a copy of that data, together with the information set out in Article 15 of the GDPR.
  • Rectification — to have inaccurate personal data corrected and incomplete personal data completed (Article 16).
  • Erasure — to have your personal data deleted where one of the grounds in Article 17 applies, including where it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or where you withdraw consent and there is no other legal basis.
  • Restriction — to have our processing of your personal data restricted in the circumstances described in Article 18.
  • Portability — to receive personal data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and to transmit it to another controller, where processing is based on consent or contract and carried out by automated means (Article 20).
  • Objection — to object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests (Article 21(1)), and to object at any time and without justification to processing for direct-marketing purposes (Article 21(2)), after which we will cease such processing.
  • Withdrawal of consent — to withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent (Article 7(3)).
  • Complaint — to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country of habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement (Article 77), or, in the United Kingdom, with the Information Commissioner's Office. We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concerns before you do so.

Where you exercise a right under this Section, we will respond without undue delay and in any event within one month of receipt, which we may extend by up to two further months where the request is complex or numerous, in which case we will inform you of the extension and the reasons for it. We do not charge a fee for responding unless your request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

13. How to Exercise Your Rights; Verification and Appeals

To exercise any right described in this Policy, contact us at privacy@hoververse.ai or by mail using the details in Section 19, and state the right you wish to exercise and the jurisdiction in which you are located. You may use an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf where applicable law permits; we may require proof of the agent's authority.

We will take reasonable steps to verify your identity before responding, which may require you to confirm information already in our records. We will not require you to create an account with us in order to submit a request, and we will use information you provide for verification only for that purpose. We will respond to requests under the TDPSA and comparable U.S. state laws within forty-five (45) days of receipt, and may extend that period once by a further forty-five (45) days where reasonably necessary, in which case we will notify you of the extension and the reason for it within the initial response period. We will respond to requests under PIPEDA within thirty (30) days, and to requests under the GDPR within the period described in Section 12.

If we decline to act on your request, we will tell you why. If you are a Texas resident or a resident of another U.S. state whose privacy law provides an appeal right, you may appeal that decision within a reasonable period by writing to privacy@hoververse.ai with the subject line “Privacy Request Appeal.” We will inform you in writing of the outcome of the appeal, and the reasons for it, within sixty (60) days of receipt. If we deny your appeal, we will provide you with an online mechanism or other method to submit a complaint to the Texas Attorney General at www.texasattorneygeneral.gov, or to the attorney general or supervisory authority of your state.

14. International Transfers of Personal Information

We are based in the United States and Canada, and our service providers may process personal information in those and other countries. As a result, your personal information may be transferred to, stored in, and accessed from a jurisdiction whose data-protection laws differ from those of your own.

Where we transfer personal data out of the EEA or the United Kingdom to a country that has not received an adequacy decision from the European Commission or, as applicable, the United Kingdom government, we rely on appropriate safeguards under Chapter V of the GDPR, principally the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and, for United Kingdom transfers, the International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK Addendum to those clauses), supplemented by transfer impact assessments and additional technical and organizational measures where our assessment indicates they are needed. Canada benefits from a partial adequacy decision of the European Commission with respect to commercial organizations subject to PIPEDA. You may request a copy of the safeguards we rely on by contacting us.

Where we transfer personal information outside Canada, we remain accountable for it under PIPEDA and use contractual means to provide a comparable level of protection while it is being processed by a third party. Where we transfer personal information outside Quebec, we conduct a privacy impact assessment as Law 25 requires before doing so.

15. Retention of Personal Information

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, and thereafter only as required to comply with our legal, accounting, or reporting obligations or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. In practice, we retain inquiry and demonstration-request records for twenty-four (24) months after our last substantive communication with you; marketing-contact and consent records for thirty-six (36) months after your last engagement with our communications, or until you opt out or withdraw consent, whichever is earlier; and Site analytics and cookie data for the retention period stated in our cookie preference tool, which does not exceed twenty-six (26) months. When personal information is no longer needed, we delete, destroy, or irreversibly anonymize it in accordance with our records-retention schedule.

16. Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against loss, theft, and unauthorized access, disclosure, copying, use, or modification, appropriate to the sensitivity of the information. These measures include encryption of personal information in transit and at rest, role-based access controls and least-privilege provisioning, network and endpoint monitoring, vulnerability management and patching, personnel confidentiality obligations and privacy training, vendor security due diligence, and an incident-response plan that is tested periodically. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a personal-data breach, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority and affected individuals where and within the timeframes that applicable law requires, including Article 33 and Article 34 of the GDPR, PIPEDA, Quebec's Law 25, and the Texas Identity Theft Enforcement and Protection Act.

17. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for business users and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of thirteen (13), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a minor under the age of eighteen (18) for purposes of targeted advertising, sale, or profiling. Where the GDPR applies and processing is based on consent, we do not knowingly offer information-society services directly to a child below the age specified by the applicable Member State (between thirteen and sixteen years) without the consent of the holder of parental responsibility. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@hoververse.ai and we will delete it.

18. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy from time to time. Revised versions will be posted on the Site with an updated “Last Updated” date and will be effective when posted. Where a change materially affects how we handle personal information already collected, we will provide additional notice and, where applicable law requires, obtain your consent before the change applies to that information. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.
