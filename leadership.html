Chief Operating Officer

Technology commercialization veteran; co-inventor and joint holder of the company's core provisional intellectual property. Strategic focus: building the go-to-market engine that carries Hover OS into mainstream enterprise adoption — while mentoring founders and early-stage teams along the way.

Chief Technology Officer

Strategic focus: decoupling spatial computing from resource-heavy, high-latency cloud architectures — proving deterministic, privacy-first math outperforms legacy neural networks at the true physical edge.
