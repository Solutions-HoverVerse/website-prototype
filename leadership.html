Core executive leadership

The team leading Physical AI for the built environment.

DL

David Lowe

President & Head of Solutions

Enterprise growth executive with two decades scaling technology and media companies — helped build L90's business development function ahead of its $100M NASDAQ IPO, and has closed enterprise partnerships with Intel, Hilton, Coca-Cola and Charles Schwab. Former President & CEO of Southpaw Technology and a graduate of the UCLA Anderson School of Management Executive Program; leads corporate vision, enterprise business development and commercialization strategy at Hoververse.

Strategic focus: replacing legacy, high-friction hardware touchpoints with zero-latency spatial environments — translating proprietary tracking into quantifiable ROI for global enterprise markets.

JC

Jeff Campbell

Chief Operating Officer

Technology commercialization veteran, co-inventor alongside lead inventor Dmitrii Paniukov, Hoververse's core intellectual property, and muti startup-exit operator: grew AssetMetrix into a $10M business, then acquired by Microsoft, then served at Microsoft as Director of Product Management before leading Perspecsys as CEO, closing an $8M Series A that set the stage for its acquisition by Blue Coat Systems — later absorbed by Symantec in a $4.65B cybersecurity deal. Leads business strategy, platform and product management, bringing Hover OS to market.

Strategic focus: building the go-to-market engine that carries Hover OS into mainstream enterprise adoption — while mentoring founders and early-stage teams along the way.

DP

Dmitrii Paniukov, PhD

Chief Technology Officer

Deep-tech engineer, systems architect and lead inventor of Hoververse's core intellectual property, leading Hoververse's technical roadmap, core development team and defensible algorithm design; holds a PhD in Psychological Sciences from Texas Tech University, with a research and engineering background spanning machine learning, neurophysiologic analysis, low-latency edge operating environments and hardware-agnostic platform architecture.

Strategic focus: decoupling spatial computing from resource-heavy, high-latency cloud architectures — proving deterministic, privacy-first math outperforms legacy neural networks at the true physical edge.

NW

Nicolas Wisniewski

VP Systems Engineering

Specialist in human-machine interaction and complex hardware retrofitting, translating proprietary computer-vision software into repeatable, site-specific field deployments; brings hands-on solution-implementation experience managing the technical nuances of installing physical technology into live commercial environments.

Strategic focus: transforming cutting-edge computer vision into seamless, natural user habits — ensuring the aim, hold, commit workflow operates flawlessly in high-traffic environments.
