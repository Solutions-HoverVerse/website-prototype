Hoververse / Solutions

One platform. Built for the spaces people share.

Every sector below shares the same failure point: a shared touch surface — a keyboard, a touchscreen, a kiosk — that spreads infection, breaks a sterile field, wears out a fragile asset, or simply can't see what's happening in front of it. Hoververse removes that surface entirely and gives each sector back the safety, uptime or visibility it was missing. SurfaceWare™ and Hover OS are the platform underneath every application below — the reason we can promise this works in your environment, not just in a lab. Healthcare remains our primary focus, and we're looking for the right health-system, retail and public-sector partners to co-develop the rest with us.
