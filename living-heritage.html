Hoververse / Solutions / Living Heritage

Immersive engagement that never touches the collection.

Museums and cultural venues face a hard tradeoff: deepen visitor engagement, or protect fragile environments and irreplaceable objects. Living Heritage removes the tradeoff — heads-up, open-air interaction that disappears into the architecture when idle, so nothing is mounted to a wall, bolted to a case, or within reach of a visitor's hand.

Living Heritage · three modules

Immersive spatial narratives without architectural compromise.

Tech that disappears into the architecture.

Heads-up experiences for museums, galleries and high-traffic public venues — open-air gestures on historic walls, glass and gallery floors, with projections that emerge on proximity and leave sightlines intact when idle. Deployed independently or together.

Photo placeholder · visitor at interpretive wall
Engage

Living Heritage | Engage

Static placards and fixed screens go unread and permanently occupy the wall. Engage replaces them with projection-mapped interpretive content that appears on approach and disappears when idle — deeper engagement with the sightlines preserved.

Photo placeholder · wayfinding on glass
Guide

Living Heritage | Guide

Printed maps and static wayfinding signage can't adapt to a visitor's actual position or accessibility needs. Guide projects a real-time Blue Dot and 3D stacked maps directly onto glass, walls and floors instead — built on deep Mappedin SDK integration.

Photo placeholder · digital twin overlay
Extend

Living Heritage | Extend

A venue's reach ends at its front doors — anyone who can't travel there simply never experiences it. Extend anchors high-fidelity digital twins to physical locations instead, opening the collection to remote, global exploration.
