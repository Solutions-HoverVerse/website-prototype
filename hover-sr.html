Retail · Hover SR (Sentient Retail)

Convert passive environments into intent-driven transaction engines.

Two applications, one platform: measurement and commerce.

Online stores track every hover, click and abandoned cart. Physical stores see only who walks in and what rings up. Hover SR is the application we're building on the platform to close that gap — turning the store into a live, interactive web page that captures the "invisible middle" of the in-store journey and translates real-world behavior into standard e-commerce funnel analytics. Anonymously.

Application 01 · Sentient analytics

E-commerce precision, offline

Measure physical click-through, product consideration time and in-aisle cart abandonment — then trigger real-time, on-shelf cross-sell.

Anonymous & compliant

Reads abstract radio-wave and volumetric signals — no facial recognition, no MAC tracking. Safe under GDPR and CCPA.

Application 02 · Touchless interactive commerce

Storefront glass and display windows go dark the moment the store closes — dead retail space that only converts customers already inside. A compact, outward-facing RealSense depth camera turns them into contact-free "aim, hold, commit" checkout engines instead — no touchscreen, no app download, no friction, live around the clock.

Legacy display conversion

The outward-facing RealSense depth camera retrofits onto existing non-interactive displays and video walls — turning them into fully interactive touchless nodes with no new terminal hardware.

Daylight-robust through glass

Direct sunlight and thick storefront glass defeat most depth cameras. Ours is calibrated to hold tracking accuracy through both.

24/7 off-hours commercialization

Safely turns external-facing windows into secure, interactive shopping nodes around the clock — no truck rolls to deploy.
