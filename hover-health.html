Touchless clinical workstations

Modernize the highest-friction surface first.

Retrofit the Workstation on Wheels — no hardware overhaul.

WOWs are the highest-frequency touchpoint in the hospital — shared by every clinician on every shift, and a primary vector for cross-contamination. Gloved or occupied hands also mean slower charting at the exact moment care can't wait. This is the application we're designing as a low-CAPEX retrofit for existing mobile carts and diagnostic screens, powered by Hover OS: clinicians navigate charts and review imaging by natural gesture and voice, without ever touching the cart.

Patient rooms & nursing stations

Unified touchless control

Shared dashboards and room-environment panels get touched by every hand on every shift change. SurfaceWare™ turns them into gesture-enabled navigation hubs instead — patient education, room controls and coordination boards, managed heads-up without ever touching the surface.

Contactless rehabilitation analytics

Therapy as a data-rich space

Manual range-of-motion measurement means touching healing grafts and strapping on patient-worn markers. Markerless depth vision removes both — measuring gait, ROM and limb extension with no contact, so burn and PT patients stay protected while a gamified reaching wall keeps them engaged.

Clinical Gesture Companion

Eyes on the patient

Confirming an ambient-scribe chart edit today means breaking eye contact to type. The Clinical Gesture Companion pairs voice with a glance-and-point gesture instead — clinicians keep their eyes on the patient, correlated on-device with no personal video retained.
