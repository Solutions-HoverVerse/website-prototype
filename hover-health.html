Touchless clinical workstations

Modernize the highest-friction surface first.

Retrofit the Workstation on Wheels — no hardware overhaul.

WOWs are the high-frequency engines of hospital documentation — and a primary vector for cross-contamination. Powered by Hover OS, our solution is a low-CAPEX retrofit for existing mobile carts and diagnostic screens: clinicians navigate charts and review imaging by natural gesture and voice, gloved or occupied, without physical contact.

Patient rooms & nursing stations

Unified touchless control

SurfaceWare™ turns fixed monitors and care-team dashboards into gesture-enabled navigation hubs — patient education, room-environment controls and coordination boards, managed heads-up without surface contact during rapid review or shift changes.

Contactless rehabilitation analytics

Therapy as a data-rich space

Markerless depth vision measures range of motion, gait and limb extension with no patient-worn markers, straps or skin-contact sensors — protecting healing grafts. Ideal for burn recovery and physical therapy; a gamified reaching wall keeps patients engaged.

Clinical Gesture Companion

Eyes on the patient

Pairs an ambient voice scribe with gesture: a clinician glances at a projected display and points to confirm a chart edit — keeping eye contact. Correlated on-device, with no personal video retained.

Controlled environments · biosafety & cleanrooms

Where every touchpoint is a potential breach.

Preserve the sterile field, end the barrier economy.

In BSL-3/BSL-4 labs and ISO Class 1–9 cleanrooms, the SurfaceWare™ engine tracks hands through containment sashes — so the laminar air curtain is never broken to reach a mouse or keyboard.

✓

End the disposable barrier economy

Compliance-first architecture

Cleanroom-safe housings and local, on-device processing that feeds securely into your lab system. Designed to meet FDA 21 CFR Part 11 and ISO/IEC 17025 validation requirements.
