Physical AI · spatial computing for the built environment

Architecture becomes the interface.

Hoververse transforms walls, glass, counters, displays and public spaces into intelligent touchless interfaces — the SurfaceWare UI (SUI), our alternative to the legacy mouse-and-screen GUI. SurfaceWare™ senses the gesture; Hover OS renders the content it acts on. No screens to touch. No devices to wear. No training required.

Physical AI is unfolding across three frontiers: device-based personal Physical AI, robotic Physical AI, and Physical AI for the built environment. Hoververse leads this third pillar — using patented computer vision and edge computing to turn ordinary architectural surfaces into intelligent, touchless interfaces. It's ambient computing and Spatial AI applied to shared physical space — no headset, no wearable, no personal device.

One natural motion

Aim. Hold. Commit.

Every Hoververse experience is built around a simple, gesture-based computing model that people understand instinctively. Natural gestures are the only input — Hover OS reads each one, determines intent, and translates it into action.

01 — INPUT

Aim

Point naturally toward content. Nothing to hold, nothing to wear, nothing to learn.

02 — INTENT

Hold

Hover OS recognizes intent, confirming explicit attention — no tap, no instructions, no training.

03 — ACTION

Commit

The action executes instantly and contact-free — driving your existing app exactly as a select would. Every gesture can also be captured as an analytics signal or automation trigger.

Learn nothing

No instructions. No controller. No app. No touchscreen.

Why Hoververse

Heads-up, not screen-down.

Vector
Legacy / screen-first systems
Hoververse framework
UX focus
Forces visitors to look down at personal screens or glowing interactive portals — fracturing the social space.
Heads-up engagement. Projections blend into architecture, emerging only on proximity and leaving the space intact when idle.
AI model
Probabilistic deep-learning scenes — variable output, high latency from cloud round-trips.
Deterministic local CV. Exact, predictable output with zero internet lag and lower total cost of ownership.
Maintenance
Fixed camera setups requiring frequent manual recalibration, spiking service costs.
Automatic calibration. Surfaces auto-detect and mapping adjusts in real time to environmental shifts.
