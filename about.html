HoverVerse / About

Reimagining human interaction.

HoverVerse is a Physical AI company building the next generation of touchless human-computer interaction. Our technology transforms ordinary architectural surfaces into intelligent digital interfaces that people can use naturally through gesture, voice and presence.

From hospitals and museums to retail environments and public spaces, we help organizations create safer, more engaging and more accessible experiences.

Who we serve

Four ways organizations put surfaces to work.

Engage audiences

Deliver immersive, touchless experiences that capture attention and deepen visitor connection.

Dynamic environments

Train teams and present information in responsive, interactive spaces.

Spatial showcases

Display products and digital assets in a whole new physical dimension.

Hygiene & accessibility

Enhance safety in high-traffic spaces by eliminating shared-surface contact vectors entirely.
