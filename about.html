Hoververse / About

Reimagining human interaction.

We believe architecture itself should respond to human intent — no screen to find, no device to carry, no training required. Hoververse turns ordinary walls, glass, counters and displays into intelligent digital interfaces that people use naturally, through gesture, voice and presence.

From hospitals and museums to retail environments and public spaces, we help organizations create safer, more engaging and more accessible experiences — replacing the shared keyboard, mouse and touchscreen with something better.

The 3 Pillars of Physical AI

The market we exist to serve.

Physical AI is unfolding across three distinct frontiers — and we build in exactly one of them. Personal Physical AI (handheld devices) and robotic Physical AI (mobile machines) are being built by others; we don't compete there. We're focused entirely on the third pillar — Physical AI for the built environment — an unserved market where spatial intelligence is embedded directly into walls, glass, counters, and architecture, with no personal screens or wearable devices required.

PILLAR 01

Device-Based Personal Physical AI

Smartphones, wearables, and vision headsets focused on individual digital productivity.

Built by others — not Hoververse
PILLAR 02

Robotic Physical AI

Autonomous humanoids, drones, and mobile robotics navigating physical motion and spatial logistics.

Built by others — not Hoververse
PILLAR 03 · HOVERVERSE

Built Environment Physical AI

Ambient computing and Spatial AI — device-free spatial computing that turns physical architecture into contact-free interactive surfaces.

Our purpose

Vision, mission and values.

Vision

To create cleaner, safer and more efficient environments for people to interact with computing in a more natural, hygienic and touchless manner.

Mission

Our mission is to make computer interaction natural and free of physical devices — no keyboards, no mice, no touchscreens.

Human-Centricity

Touchless, accessible, and built with human needs at the core of every interaction we design.

Robust Learning

AI-powered experiences that evolve with every interaction, using data to build increasingly adaptable systems.

Adaptability & Evolution

Flexible, scalable solutions ready for any space, surface or challenge — embracing change as the market and technology shift.

Collaborative Partnerships

We partner across industries to solve real-world problems together, building sustainable relationships with clients and partners.

Data-Driven Excellence

We use data to drive excellence, reduce bias, and create measurable impact in everything we build.
